## Seasonal Considerations

### Best Departure Windows:
- **Spring (March-May)**: Avoid extreme heat, good weather through most of route
- **Fall (September-November)**: Similar benefits, cooler temps
- **Summer**: Avoid I-40 through Texas/New Mexico (95°F+)
- **Winter**: Possible snow in Arkansas/Oklahoma mountains, avoid if possible

### Monsoon Season (July-Sept):
- Heavy afternoons in Arizona/New Mexico
- Plan indoor work buffer days
- Monitor weather daily
